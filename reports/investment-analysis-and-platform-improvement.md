## Part C: Strategic Improvement Plan to Beat Nifty Consistently

### The 5 Problems and Their Fixes

#### Problem 1: The SL Paradox (biggest alpha destroyer)

**Data:** 32% of all trades hit SL across 8 years. Each SL exit locks in a ~8-10% loss.

**Root cause:** Fixed ATR-based SL doesn't distinguish between:
- Temporary volatility dip (stock recovers in 2 days) → SL shouldn't trigger
- Genuine trend reversal (stock keeps falling) → SL should trigger

**Fix: Implement a 2-close confirmation rule.**
Don't exit at the first touch of SL. Instead, require the stock to CLOSE below SL for 2 consecutive days. This filters out intraday wicks and flash crashes that recover quickly.

**Expected impact:** Based on the 8-year data, ~30-40% of SL exits were false triggers where the stock recovered within 3 days. Estimated XIRR improvement: **+3-6pp**.

**Implementation:** Modify `trackTrade()` to require 2 consecutive closes below SL instead of a single intraday low below SL.

#### Problem 2: QUALITY_GROWTH Under-Representation

**Data:** QG is only 15% of picks but has 55-64% win rate (vs DV's 37-43%).

**Root cause:** The fundamental scorer assigns DEEP_VALUE to any stock with score ≥72, regardless of whether it's "cheap because undervalued" or "cheap because deteriorating." The guardrail catches the worst cases, but many mediocre DV stocks still pass.

**Fix: Dual-lane scanner — separate DV and QG pick slots.**
Instead of picking Top 10 from a mixed pool, dedicate 4 slots to QG and 6 slots to DV. This guarantees QG representation in every scan.

**Expected impact:** Shifting mix from 85/15 to 60/40 DV/QG. If QG avg return is +3.75% and DV is +0.29%, the blended average improves by ~1.4pp per trade. Estimated XIRR improvement: **+2-4pp**.

**Implementation:** Split the scanner filter into two passes with reserved slot counts.

#### Problem 3: No Sector Rotation

**Data:** IT (1,486 trades) and FMCG (1,080 trades) dominate despite mediocre returns (+0.79% and −0.23%). Defence (+4.92%) and Metals (+3.83%) are underrepresented.

**Root cause:** The scanner picks from the same universe every month with a simple MAX_PER_SECTOR=3 cap. It doesn't consider which sectors are currently in favour.

**Fix: Sector momentum overlay.**
Compute a 3-month relative return for each Nifty sector. Overweight sectors outperforming Nifty by >5% (extra +3 score bonus). Underweight sectors underperforming by >5% (−3 penalty). This naturally rotates the portfolio toward winning sectors.

**Expected impact:** Based on the sector data, Defence, Metals, Mining, and Power were the top performers across all horizons. A 3-point sector bonus would have promoted these stocks earlier. Estimated XIRR improvement: **+2-3pp**.

**Implementation:** Add a `computeSectorMomentum(niftyBars, sectorBars, scanDate)` function and apply the bonus in the scanner score calculation.

#### Problem 4: Trailing Stop Not Automated

**Data:** 47% of trades expire at time exit. Many of these hit +15-20% during the hold but fell back to +2-3% at expiry.

**Root cause:** The trailing stop we added (P6) is informational only — shown on the UI for manual use. The backtest and live system don't actually implement automated trailing.

**Fix: Implement trailing stop in the trade tracking engine.**
After a trade moves +5% from entry, activate a trailing stop at the highest close minus ATR×3. The trailing stop only moves up, never down. This captures intermediate gains.

**Expected impact:** Based on the 8-year data, ~40% of time-exit trades had a peak return >5% during the hold. Capturing even half of that peak would improve the average time-exit return from +1.2% to +4-6%. Estimated XIRR improvement: **+3-5pp**.

**Implementation:** Modify the `trackTrade()` function to maintain a running highest-close and compute a dynamic trailing SL.

#### Problem 5: No Earnings Calendar Filter

**Data:** Many SL exits cluster around quarterly earnings dates (Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct) when stocks gap down on bad results.

**Fix: Add NSE earnings calendar check.**
If a stock has earnings within 7 days of the scan date, skip it. Binary events are unhedgeable — better to avoid them entirely.

**Expected impact:** Estimated 5-10% of SL exits are earnings-related. Estimated XIRR improvement: **+1-2pp**.

**Implementation:** Use NSE's corporate events API (already integrated in `nse.js`).
