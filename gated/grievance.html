Grievance Procedure

How to raise a concern about anything you've seen, read, or acted on through the Starbhai platform.

Scope

Starbhai is an educational research platform built and maintained by a single person. It is not a SEBI-registered Research Analyst or Investment Adviser, so the formal SCORES grievance route does not apply. However, at the friends-and-family tier, every concern is treated as a personal matter and answered directly by the author.

What to file a grievance about

  • A recommendation that materially misled you — wrong direction, wrong scoring inputs, factually incorrect rationale.
  • A bug that resulted in stale data being shown as fresh, or a stop-loss / sell-trigger value computed incorrectly.
  • A privacy concern — your portfolio data was exposed, or another user could see your holdings.
  • A conflict-of-interest concern — the author appeared to favour a stock they held without disclosing it on the holdings disclosure.
  • Anything else that, on reflection, the platform should not have done.

How to file

Email mtaluja11@gmail.com with subject line Starbhai grievance. Include:

  1. The recommendation or screen that triggered the concern (URL, ticker, or screenshot).
  2. The date / time you acted on it (if applicable).
  3. The concrete harm or risk (financial impact, privacy concern, data accuracy).
  4. What outcome you'd like — fix, retraction, clarification, refund (if applicable).

What to expect

  • Acknowledgement within 24 hours. If you don't get one, your mail likely went to spam — re-send from a different address.
  • Initial response within 5 working days with the author's diagnosis and proposed remediation.
  • Closure within 30 days. Substantive grievances result in a public-facing post-incident note attached to the holdings disclosure file, so others see the lesson learned.

Escalation

There is no second-level escalation route (the author is also the platform maintainer). If a grievance is unresolved to your satisfaction after 30 days, you are welcome to share the matter with peers in the friends-and-family group directly.

What this is not

This grievance procedure does NOT create an advisor-client relationship. Starbhai does not issue personalised investment advice, does not handle client money, and does not have fiduciary obligations under SEBI IA / RA regulations. The content of the platform remains educational research, with all the limitations set out in the site footer.
