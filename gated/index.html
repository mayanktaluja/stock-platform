Fundamental Value Scanner

Quality-adjusted value — 9-dimension score blending valuation, profitability, leverage & growth ·

ⓘ About this scanner: Each stock is scored 0–100 across nine dimensions organised into five pillars: Valuation (P/E vs sector median, absolute P/E, 52-week position), Profitability (ROE, net profit margin), Financial Health (debt-to-equity), Growth (YoY revenue growth), and Trend context (price vs 200-DMA, market-cap tier). A stock cannot be labelled Deep Value or Quality Growth unless its ROE and leverage both clear the quality guardrail — this prevents value-trap picks where a cheap P/E masks a deteriorating business. The Buy Now scanner uses a dual-lane system: 4 slots for Quality Growth and 1 for Deep Value (DV must clear ROE ≥ 15% and adjusted score ≥ 70 — a tighter bar than QG's ≥ 65, because 24-month attribution showed DV had −1.97% edge across 193 trades vs QG's +1.97% across 47). The pipeline applies a sector momentum overlay (sectors beating Nifty by >3% get +3, lagging by >3% get −3), a 3-trading-day earnings blackout (the 7-day window is flagged on the card), and a max 2 picks per sector diversification cap. Tourism, Cement, and Chemicals are excluded based on the 2025 backtest (consistently negative attribution); Banking was kept in after the same diagnostic showed it would have contributed +5.07% avg return over 29 trades during the rate-cut cycle. All picks include a 2-close SL confirmation rule and a dynamic trailing stop to lock in gains. ROE, debt, margin, and growth data are sourced from Yahoo Finance; prices and sector P/E from NSE India.

Track Record

Forward returns on every SWS pick — measured against the Nifty 50 benchmark. Snapshots taken when each pipeline run publishes picks-latest.json; positions are closed when a section drops them.

ⓘ How this works: Every SWS pipeline run (which writes data/sws/picks-latest.json) snapshots all 10 sections — Top 30, Best Buy Now, Deep Value, Quality Growth, Mid-term, Dividend Aristocrats, Small-Cap Gems, Insider Buying, Upcoming Earnings, and Avoid — into the trade log, with the file's own scanned_at as the recommendation timestamp. Forward return is the % change from snapshot price to current price (or to the close price, when a section drops the ticker), compared against how the Nifty 50 moved over the same window. Beats Nifty means the pick generated alpha — outperformed the index. The Avoid list inverts: a "win" is the pick under-performing Nifty, as we predicted. Aim for ≥55% beats-Nifty rate over 30+ days to validate the platform's signals.
