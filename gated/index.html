Track Record

Forward returns on every SWS pick — measured against the Nifty 50 benchmark. Snapshots taken when each pipeline run publishes picks-latest.json; positions are closed when a section drops them.

ⓘ How this works: Every SWS pipeline run (which writes data/sws/picks-latest.json) snapshots all 10 sections — Top 30, Best Buy Now, Deep Value, Quality Growth, Mid-term, Dividend Aristocrats, Small-Cap Gems, Insider Buying, Upcoming Earnings, and Avoid — into the trade log, with the file's own scanned_at as the recommendation timestamp. Forward return is the % change from snapshot price to current price (or to the close price, when a section drops the ticker), compared against how the Nifty 50 moved over the same window. Beats Nifty means the pick generated alpha — outperformed the index. The Avoid list inverts: a "win" is the pick under-performing Nifty, as we predicted. Aim for ≥55% beats-Nifty rate over 30+ days to validate the platform's signals.

i Methodology — how these numbers are computed click to expand

1. Holding periods. Every pick is tracked at 1 month / 3 months / 6 months / 12 months after snapshot. Earnings-event picks (BEAT / MISS) are tracked only at T+1 (next trading day's close) because the signal decays immediately.

2. Alpha formula. For each pick at each horizon: α = pick_return_pct − benchmark_return_pct. A positive α means the pick outperformed its benchmark over the same window.

3. Benchmark routing by market cap. Large-cap picks are benchmarked against Nifty 50 TRI; mid-caps against Nifty Midcap 150 TRI; small-caps against Nifty Smallcap 250 TRI. Total Return Index (TRI) includes dividends so the comparison is apples-to-apples.

4. Drop-out handling. When a section stops recommending a pick (e.g. score falls below threshold), the position is closed at the price the section dropped it — not at zero. Closed picks remain in the ledger and contribute to the Hit Rate / Avg α numbers (no survivor bias).

5. Snapshot timing. The recommendation timestamp is the scanned_at field from picks-latest.json (when the SWS pipeline finished its run) — NOT the next session's open. This is the most conservative attribution and avoids look-ahead bias.
