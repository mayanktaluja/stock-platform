Investor Charter

Plain-English commitments and explicit limitations. Modelled on SEBI's Investor Charter convention even though this platform is not formally registered as a Research Analyst or Investment Adviser.

What this platform promises

  1. Honest disclosures. Every scoring decision is auditable in the methodology document. The author's personal positions and third-party data relationships are published at /api/disclosures/holdings and rotated quarterly.
  2. Honest backtest reporting. Hit-rate and Brier score are computed on archived predictions with walk-forward cross-validation and bootstrap 95% confidence intervals. The platform refuses to claim a confidence-cap lift until the sample-size + bucket-hit-rate + Brier gate clears.
  3. Hard gates before recommendations. The portfolio analyser refuses to score until the 3-question risk profile is complete (not "advisable", required).
  4. Concrete sell triggers. Every position gets a rupee-priced stop, not just an "advisory drawdown" label.
  5. NSE Surveillance respect. Stocks under ASM/GSM are loudly flagged and EXCLUDED from picks, regardless of score.
  6. Grievance response. 24-hour acknowledgement, 5-working-day initial response, 30-day closure. See /legal/grievance for the full procedure.
  7. No paid placements. No third-party data provider compensates the author for inclusion or favourable scoring (see disclosure for relationship details).

What this platform explicitly does NOT do

  1. Issue personalised investment advice. Recommendations are educational research, not advice tailored to your specific financial situation, goals, or tax position.
  2. Handle client money or securities. The platform never takes custody of funds or holdings. There is no demat link, no order routing, no transaction execution.
  3. Guarantee returns or hit-rate. Past performance — including the calibration plot on the Track Record tab — does not guarantee future results. Markets move; methodology has limits.
  4. Operate as a SEBI-registered Research Analyst or Investment Adviser. The formal SCORES grievance route does not apply. Disputes are handled directly with the author via the grievance procedure.
  5. Subscribe to or charge a fee. The platform is unmonetised and operates at the friends-and-family scale. There is no commercial relationship between the author and any user.
  6. Onboard arbitrary users. Access is gated behind Google OAuth + an admin allow-list. Coverage stops at the people the author knows.

Your rights as a user

  • To see the basis for any recommendation — check the methodology, the per-component breakdown on the Earnings Watch card, or the audit-trail endpoint if curious.
  • To export your portfolio data — uploaded files are stored per-user and downloadable on request.
  • To delete your data — email the author with subject "Data deletion" and your account email. Deletion completes within 7 working days.
  • To file a grievance — see /legal/grievance.
  • To opt out at any time — sign out and stop using the platform; no further obligation.

Update cadence

This Charter is reviewed quarterly. Material changes (new commitments removed, scope changes) are emailed directly to active users at the previous-quarter's review cycle. The methodology document is updated whenever scoring weights change or new components are added.
