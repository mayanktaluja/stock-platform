Fundamental Value Scanner

Quality-adjusted value — 9-dimension score blending valuation, profitability, leverage & growth ·

ⓘ About this scanner: Each stock is scored 0–100 across nine dimensions organised into five pillars: Valuation (P/E vs sector median, absolute P/E, 52-week position), Profitability (ROE, net profit margin), Financial Health (debt-to-equity), Growth (YoY revenue growth), and Trend context (price vs 200-DMA, market-cap tier). A stock cannot be labelled Deep Value or Quality Growth unless its ROE and leverage both clear the quality guardrail — this prevents value-trap picks where a cheap P/E masks a deteriorating business. The Buy Now scanner uses a dual-lane system: 6 slots reserved for Quality Growth (55-64% win rate) and 4 for Deep Value, with sector momentum overlay (outperforming sectors get priority) and earnings calendar filter (stocks with results within 7 days are flagged). Banking, Tourism, Cement, and Chemicals sectors are excluded based on 8-year backtesting (consistently negative returns). All picks include a 2-close SL confirmation rule and a dynamic trailing stop to lock in gains. ROE, debt, margin, and growth data are sourced from Yahoo Finance; prices and sector P/E from NSE India.
